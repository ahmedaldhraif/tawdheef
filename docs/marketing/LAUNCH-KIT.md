# Tawdheef launch kit

Prepared 14 November 2026. These are drafts for Ahmed AlDhraif's own channels. Nothing in this file has been posted or sent.

Use the approved Tawdheef name, wordmark and real product assets. The links below go to the public GitHub project. Use Tawdheef.com links after the corresponding public pages and redirects have been verified. Tabbio remains the separate CV integration; the product and contribution invitation stay under Tawdheef.

## Before posting

- Recheck the linked download and current setup guide. Source, website and installed app versions can differ.
- Use public directory data or isolated demonstration records. Keep CVs, mailboxes, credentials and private activity out of recordings.
- Show only observed behavior. A filled form, created account or uploaded CV is not a confirmed application.
- When sharing a recording, attach the actual reviewed clip. The agent-task draft below also works as a text post without claiming a recording exists.
- Treat replies, screenshots, contributor quotes and results as material to review before any later publication. These drafts do not authorise posting or messaging.

## 1. Introduce the project

### English

I built Tawdheef to give job seekers and their agents one place to work.

It's a free, open-source app for finding matching jobs, building career profiles and tracking applications on your computer.

Start with a company directory, use it with Codex or Claude Code, and keep a record of what actually happened. You can explore the public directories before installing anything.

Run currently needs a connected Tabbio CV. Public research and manual tracking work without one. Your AI account and connected services may have separate costs.

Found a missing company? Have a directory for your country? You can help improve it.

Start here: https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef

### العربية

بنيت Tawdheef ليكون مكانًا واحدًا يعمل فيه الباحث عن عمل مع مساعده.

تطبيق مجاني ومفتوح المصدر للبحث عن وظائف مناسبة، وإنشاء ملفات مهنية ومتابعة طلبات التوظيف على جهازك.

ابدأ بدليل شركات، واستخدمه مع Codex أو Claude Code، واحتفظ بسجل لما تم فعلًا. ويمكنك استكشاف الأدلة العامة قبل تثبيت التطبيق.

يحتاج Run حاليًا إلى سيرة مرتبطة من Tabbio. أما البحث في الأدلة والمتابعة اليدوية فيعملان دونها. قد تكون لحساب مساعدك والخدمات المرتبطة تكاليف مستقلة.

وجدت شركة ناقصة؟ لديك دليل لدولتك؟ يمكنك المساهمة في تحسينه.

ابدأ هنا: https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef

## 2. Share the public directories

### English

Looking for companies to research in the GCC?

I've shared company and recruitment-source directories for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain through Tawdheef.

Use them to find published company websites and careers routes. They are dated research lists, not a live feed of vacancies.

You can browse the data or download a pack without installing the app or creating a CV account.

If a link has changed, or a company is missing, share a public source. Contributions from any country are welcome.

Choose a country: https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/directories/README.md

## 3. Show one useful agent task

### English

Before applying, start with a company list you can check.

I added a public research request to Tawdheef. Choose a country and sector, then ask your assistant for up to eight company leads with their public sources, dates and missing information.

It starts with the compact directory index and checks selected company websites. The assistant should say what it could read and return fewer results when the evidence is limited.

You can try this before installing Tawdheef or connecting a CV. Your assistant needs tools to read public JSON and websites.

Try the request: https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/examples/README.md

### العربية

قبل التقديم، ابدأ بقائمة شركات تستطيع التحقق منها.

أضفت في Tawdheef طلبًا جاهزًا للبحث في البيانات العامة. اختر الدولة والقطاع، واطلب من مساعدك حتى ثماني جهات مع مصادرها وتواريخ البحث والمعلومات الناقصة.

يبدأ الطلب بالفهرس المختصر، ثم يتحقق من مواقع الشركات التي اختارها. على المساعد توضيح ما استطاع قراءته وعرض نتائج أقل عندما لا تكفي الأدلة.

يمكنك التجربة قبل تثبيت Tawdheef أو ربط سيرتك. يحتاج مساعدك إلى أدوات لقراءة JSON العام ومواقع الويب.

جرّب الطلب: https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/i18n/ar/examples/README.md

### Recording outline

1. Open the public research example and name one country and sector.
2. Show the request being pasted into the assistant.
3. Show the actual sources it inspected and any access limits.
4. Review the returned leads and open one official company or careers page.
5. End at the relevant directory or app setup guide. If showing personal CV matching later, use isolated demonstration information and identify it as such.

Keep the recording focused on that one task. Do not simulate progress, results, applications or connected accounts.

## 4. Invite a useful contribution

### English

One corrected careers link can save the next job seeker time.

Tawdheef is open to contributions: fix a detail, add a company, share a directory for your country, or improve the app and guides.

You don't need to code to suggest company data. Send the organisation, a public source and the date you checked it. A small, useful correction is enough to start.

Suggestions are reviewed. Updated packs become downloadable when a new version is published.

Ways to help: https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md

### العربية

تصحيح رابط توظيف واحد قد يوفّر وقت الباحث عن عمل الذي يأتي بعدك.

المساهمة في Tawdheef مفتوحة: صحّح معلومة، أضف شركة، شارك دليلًا لدولتك أو حسّن التطبيق والأدلة.

لا تحتاج إلى البرمجة لاقتراح بيانات الشركات. أرسل اسم الجهة ومصدرًا عامًا وتاريخ التحقق. تصحيح صغير ومفيد يكفي للبداية.

تُراجع الاقتراحات، وتصبح الحزم المحدّثة متاحة للتنزيل عند نشر إصدار جديد.

طرق المساهمة: https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md

## Keep the launch useful

Lead each post with one task and one relevant destination. Answer real questions through the canonical [guides](../guides/README.md), invite evidence-backed [contributions](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), and share [pack update notes](../../directories/PACK-UPDATES.md) when an update is actually published.

Use the [growth plan](OPEN-SOURCE-GROWTH.md) for the full journey and measurement definitions. A GitHub visit or download does not establish completed setup, CV creation or a successful application. The app's current Run and CV requirements belong in the [setup guide](../guides/use-the-skill.md); a new post does not change them.

## Review useful outcomes

Record available aggregate evidence after each launch piece. No baseline or conversion rate is established yet; leave unavailable results unknown. This kit adds no analytics or tracking code.

| Question | Evidence to review | Keep separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did people find the resource? | Aggregate GitHub views/referrers and published asset downloads, where available | Views, downloads and unique people |
| Was the first task useful? | Voluntary feedback about a checked company lead or reviewed shortlist | A useful lead and an application |
| Could people finish setup? | Voluntary reports or a deliberately designed opt-in readiness measure | Download and working local setup |
| Did Tabbio help at the CV step? | Tagged visits; independently attributable signup or completed CV only if available | Visits, signups, completed CVs and connected CVs |
| Did the community improve a pack? | Reviewed contribution, real merged PR and later published pack notes | Submission, merge and released update |

Do not collect CV content, mailbox text, company targets or private tracking for this review. Any future local activation measurement needs a deliberate opt-in design. The proposed [Tabbio integration page](TABBIO-INTEGRATION-PAGE.md) explains its contextual CV step without changing the project's identity.
