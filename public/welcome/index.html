Questions before you start?

Is it really free?

Tawdheef is free and MIT licensed. Your AI client and connected providers may have their own costs. Costs and connections.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding needed. Start the source with Node.js 26 LTS, then open Connections and copy the setup request into your agent. It guides sign-in in its own client. Setup guide.

What happens when I press Run?

Run saves your selected companies and actions and copies a request. Paste it into Codex or Claude Code. Your agent uses its own tools and connections and records outcomes in Tawdheef. Sign-in, CAPTCHA and provider approvals may need you. How Run works.

What if a company has no matching job?

When you choose career profiles, your agent can create an account, fill in your details and upload your CV on supported career sites. This can make your details available for future consideration. Visibility depends on the site; it does not guarantee contact. Profiles and CV uploads are recorded separately from applications. Career profiles.

Do I need Tabbio?

The current workflow uses a Tabbio CV through your agent’s own connection. You can browse companies and track records without it. Creating, editing and publishing a CV use the client’s Tabbio tools. Connect your CV.

What does Tawdheef mean?

Tawdheef (توظيف) means employment in Arabic. The app is for job seekers anywhere. Skills overview.

Can I use it on my phone?

The website works on your phone. The app and your Codex or Claude Code agent run on your computer, where the local tracker is available by default. Local requirements.

Where does my data go?

Tracking records stay on your computer. Your actual agent client manages CV, email, calendar and browser access. Tawdheef stores references and dated capability reports, not copies of your client’s sign-in sessions. Privacy.

Can I add a country or fix a company?

Yes. Suggest a correction or contribute a pack for any country on GitHub. Include public sources. Packs are dated research lists, not live vacancies. Contribute a directory.

Still need help? Ask on GitHub

GitHub issues are public. Leave out CVs, credentials and account details.
