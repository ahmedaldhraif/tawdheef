Create or connect your CV

Create a free digital CV on Tabbio or connect your existing account, then check that Tawdheef can read it.

Tawdheef uses your connected CV to match roles to your real experience. A readable connected CV is required before Run; you can browse and organise directories without one.

If you do not have a Tabbio CV

  1. Create your free digital CV on Tabbio.
  2. Add or import your real experience, education and skills. Review and save the CV you want your agent to use.
  3. Return to Tawdheef to connect that account.

Tabbio’s free plan lets you build and share your digital CV and export a PDF. Your setup agent can help with your real information; it should ask about missing facts rather than invent them.

Connect an existing CV

  1. Open Connections → Your CV → Tabbio CV in Tawdheef.
  2. Choose Connect and complete Tabbio’s sign-in and access flow for the account you intend to use.
  3. If the app needs help identifying the CV read tool, use its assistant setup instructions. The agent can discover the available tools and configure the actual readCV mapping.
  4. Choose your Run assistant in Connections and use Check connections. This makes a fresh read of the connected CV.

If the read fails, finish any pending sign-in or access step and check again. A saved connection is not the same as a successful CV read.

Keep the current version available

Update your CV in Tabbio when your experience changes. Tawdheef’s connection reads the CV you authorise. CV editing, publishing and sharing through Tabbio MCP or CLI use a separate Tabbio connection and the tools available to your account.

Expected result

Check connections reports that your CV was read successfully. Your agent can use that information without you pasting private CV details into a directory record.
