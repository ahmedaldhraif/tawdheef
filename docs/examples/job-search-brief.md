# Your job-search brief

[العربية](../i18n/ar/examples/job-search-brief.md)

Fill only what you know. This is optional direction for [public company research](README.md), not a CV or an application. Leave uncertain details blank.

```text
Roles I want to explore:
Countries and cities:
Sectors or types of organisation:
Experience level, if useful:
Work arrangement: [on-site / hybrid / remote / flexible]
Must-haves:
Preferences:
Companies or sectors to exclude:
Questions I still need to check:
```

Keep a filled copy in your own notes or private assistant conversation. It does not need contact details, identity documents, passwords or your full CV. Do not add personal copies to public GitHub issues or pull requests.

Ask your assistant to distinguish preferences from facts and leave eligibility unknown unless supported. Sharing this brief does not authorise applications, messages, account creation or CV sharing.

For matching against your actual experience later, use [Create or connect your CV](../guides/connect-your-cv.md) and the [shortlist prompt](../guides/use-the-skill.md#prompts).
