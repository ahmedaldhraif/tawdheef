Questions before you start?

Is it really free?

Tawdheef is free and MIT licensed. Your AI client and connected providers may have their own costs. Costs and connections.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding needed. Open the app folder in Codex or Claude Code and ask your agent to set it up. Node.js 24+ is required. Setup guide.

What happens when I press Run?

Your agent works through the chosen companies using your CV and the actions you request. It records confirmations and anything that needs you. Sign-ins, CAPTCHAs and provider approvals may need your help; LinkedIn application steps stay manual. How Run works.

What if a company has no matching job?

When you choose career profiles, your agent can create an account, fill in your details and upload your CV on supported career sites. This can make your details available for future consideration. Visibility depends on the site; it does not guarantee contact. Profiles and CV uploads are recorded separately from applications. Career profiles.

Do I need Tabbio?

Run requires a connected Tabbio CV. You can browse directories and track applications without it. Tawdheef reads your CV; editing it uses a separate Tabbio connection. Connect your CV.

What does Tawdheef mean?

Tawdheef (توظيف) means employment in Arabic. The app is for job seekers anywhere. Skills overview.

Can I use it on my phone?

The website works on your phone. The app and your Codex or Claude Code agent run on your computer, where the local tracker is available by default. Local requirements.

Where does my data go?

The tracker runs on your computer, where your records and connection settings are stored. Your chosen AI, CV and email services work online. When you start a run, the actions you select determine which applications, profiles or CVs your agent sends to employers. Privacy.

Can I add a country or fix a company?

Yes. Suggest a correction or contribute a pack for any country on GitHub. Include public sources. Packs are dated research lists, not live vacancies. Contribute a directory.
