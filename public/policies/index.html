About this project.

Tawdheef is an independent open-source project maintained by Ahmed AlDhraif. The Tabbio integration connects to its separate CV service.

Free and open source

The code is MIT licensed. You can use, modify, fork and redistribute it, including commercially, while keeping the licence and copyright notice. The licence includes warranty and liability disclaimers; it does not guarantee immunity from legal claims.

Read the MIT licence · Project policy

Your data

The tracker stores your records and credentials locally. They are not encrypted by the app. Connected AI, email and CV services process data under their own terms and permissions.

The website has no first-party analytics scripts or tracking pixels. Hosting services may keep access logs. Links to Tabbio include readable referral parameters.

Privacy details

Directory sources

Directories are dated research lists, not job offers or verified vacancies. Check the source before acting. Use public recruitment information and respect the rights attached to each source.
