# Names, logos and affiliation

The Tawdheef name, `tawdheef.com` wordmark and Arabic Grid symbol identify this project. Ahmed AlDhraif is the personal maintainer. This document does not claim a registered trademark or legal clearance of the name.

## Referencing the project

You may truthfully describe, link to, review or credit Tawdheef, including saying that your work is based on its MIT code. Keep the source relationship clear and avoid suggesting that a modification, service or application is an official release or has the maintainer's approval when it does not.

For a modified public fork or hosted service, use your own name and branding to make its independent operator clear. This is a recommendation to avoid confusion, not an extra condition of the MIT code licence. Keep required copyright and permission notices even when you rebrand.

The original brand artwork's existing copyright licence is not withdrawn by this page. Where that artwork was distributed under MIT, its copyright permissions remain MIT. Copyright permission to copy artwork is distinct from permission to present an offering as the project's official product or to misrepresent affiliation. Applicable trademark rights and truthful-reference rights remain separate.
