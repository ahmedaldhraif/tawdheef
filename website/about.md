# Built for your job search.

Tawdheef (توظيف) means employment in Arabic. It is a free, open-source app to apply for jobs with your agent and keep track of applications and replies.

Created and personally maintained by [Ahmed AlDhraif](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmedaldhraif). This is an independent project, with its source on [GitHub](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef).

[Get started](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/FIRST-RUN.md)

## One place to work

Run Tawdheef on your computer with Codex or Claude Code. The app keeps your records, country packs add researched companies and careers links, and the skill teaches your agent the workflow. MCP and CLI tools use the same records as the screen.

You choose where to apply. Sign-ins, CAPTCHAs and some application steps may need you.

## Your CV, ready for your agent

Tabbio is a separate digital CV service. Create a free CV there, or connect the one you already have. Tawdheef reads your CV for Run; editing or publishing it uses a separate Tabbio connection. You can browse directories and track applications without it.

[Create your free CV](https://app.tabbio.com/?utm_source=tawdheef&utm_medium=about&utm_campaign=live_cv) or [connect your CV](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/guides/connect-your-cv.md).

## Any country is welcome

Use a [country pack](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/directories/README.md), fix a link or contribute researched data for any country through GitHub issues or pull requests. Include public sources and keep private tracking out of contributions.

[Help improve a directory](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/DIRECTORY-CONTRIBUTING.md).
