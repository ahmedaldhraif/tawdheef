# Built for your job search.

Tawdheef (توظيف) means employment in Arabic. It is a free, open-source app to find jobs that fit your CV and build career profiles for future opportunities, even when no suitable job is open.

Created and personally maintained by [Ahmed AlDhraif](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmedaldhraif). An independent open-source project, open to community contributions across the app, directory packs, skills and guides. Its source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef-auto-apply-jobs).

[Get started](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef-auto-apply-jobs/blob/main/docs/FIRST-RUN.md)

## One place to work

Run Tawdheef on your computer with Codex or Claude Code. The app keeps your records, country packs add researched companies and careers links, and the skill teaches your agent the workflow. Its built-in CLI and local stdio MCP server use the same records as the screen.

Your actual client owns CV, mail, calendar, browser and password-manager access. Choose companies and actions, then paste the copied Run request into that client. Your agent can apply to matching jobs and create or complete career profiles where available. Profiles, CV uploads and applications are tracked separately. Sign-ins, CAPTCHAs and some application steps may need you.

## Your CV, ready for your agent

Tabbio is a separate digital CV service. Create a free CV there, or connect the one you already have. Your actual client reads the CV through its own Tabbio connection and records reference metadata in Tawdheef. Editing or publishing also happens through that client’s authorized Tabbio tools. You can browse directories and track applications without it.

[Create your free CV](https://app.tabbio.com/?utm_source=tawdheef&utm_medium=about&utm_campaign=live_cv) or [connect your CV](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef-auto-apply-jobs/blob/main/docs/guides/connect-your-cv.md).

## Any country is welcome

Use a [country pack](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef-auto-apply-jobs/blob/main/directories/README.md), fix a link or contribute researched data for any country through GitHub issues or pull requests. Include public sources and keep private tracking out of contributions.
