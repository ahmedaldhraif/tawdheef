# Create or connect your CV

Your agent reads your real CV through its own Tabbio connection. Tawdheef saves reference and update metadata, not a copy of the CV text.

## Start with your existing connections

Open **Connections → Tabbio CV** and copy the request into your selected client. Reuse its existing account and connection before adding anything.

## If you do not have a Tabbio CV

Use **Create a CV in Tabbio** and complete your details there. Return to the request in your client. Tabbio is available worldwide; UAE PASS verification is optional for eligible users.

## Connect an existing CV

Your client discovers its actual Tabbio tools and reads the authorized CV. Complete sign-in and consent there. Never guess a CV ID, URL or career facts. Missing access stays Needs you.

## Keep the current version available

Edits, publishing and sharing use the client's separate Tabbio capabilities and your request. Public visibility and Open to work are separate choices, not tracker setup. Search indexing and employer responses are not guaranteed.

For file-upload forms, **Tabbio CV → Original CV for uploads** can store your selected PDF/DOCX up to 10 MB locally. Saving it does not upload or email it; your client handles authorized uploads.

## Expected result

Your client can read the current CV and report dated reference metadata. [Return to setup](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef-auto-apply-jobs/blob/main/docs/guides/use-the-skill.md#cv).
