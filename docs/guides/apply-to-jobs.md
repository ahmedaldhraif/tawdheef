# Find matching jobs and build career profiles

Choose companies and press **Run**. Your Codex or Claude Code agent looks for jobs that fit your CV and can build career profiles for future opportunities, even when no suitable job is open.

## Before you start

Have a real readable CV, a working Codex or Claude Code client and a directory with records. External CV, browser/computer, mail and password-manager access belongs to that actual client. Only supported, authorized tools can be used; Tawdheef does not create those permissions.

## Choose the work

1. Choose your assistant in **Connections**. Copy setup requests into that existing client to check the access you need.
2. Choose a directory. Use the list's **City**, **Company type** and **Status** filters to narrow it. Check individual companies if you want a smaller scope.
3. Open **Run**. It includes all matching unfinished companies across every page, or only the matching companies you checked. Review the count and actions. Use **Add a note** for role or work-location preferences.
4. Press **Run** to authorize those selected actions, then paste **Copy run request** into your existing client chat. The app waits for that client; it launches no second model or CLI.
5. Use **View progress** to see the current company, outcomes and next actions. **Stop** revokes new tracker actions; stop any remaining task in the client too. Existing results and uncertain intents remain recorded.

The two main actions are selected by default:

- **Apply to jobs that match my CV:** check current vacancies against your real experience, eligibility and preferred work locations, then apply to suitable roles.
- **Create an account or complete my profile:** when no suitable job is open, reuse or create a career account, fill your profile, upload your CV or join an offered talent pool where supported.

You can turn either choice off. Choosing profiles alone lets your agent prepare for future opportunities without applying. **Email my CV for future opportunities** is a separate optional action, off by default.

Accounts or profile steps required for a matching application are part of that application. A blocked or failed vacancy search is not evidence that no role fits, so it does not automatically trigger future-opportunity actions. Not every company offers an account, profile or CV-upload route.

## When a site needs you

Sign-ins, CAPTCHAs and identity declarations may require you to finish a step. LinkedIn application steps remain manual. A blocked company gets an actionable next step so the queue can continue with others.

The queue continues after individual blockers or errors, with no app-imposed company count limit. Later explicit runs skip confirmed applications and completed companies, and can retry unfinished work. Existing accounts and profiles are reused. An uncertain send or form submission must be checked for a receipt before retrying.

## Expected result

You see real progress for each company: an account created, a profile completed, a CV uploaded, an application confirmed, or a clear **Needs you** item. A career profile is useful even without an open job; it is recorded separately from a job application. The optional **Progress** column and reports show these outcomes separately.

Native scheduled checks read selected existing sources through the client's own tools. They do not start applications, create profiles, upload/share CVs, send messages or change calendars.
