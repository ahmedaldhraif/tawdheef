# Set up Tawdheef

Tawdheef is a free Auto Job Apply & Application Tracker that runs on your computer. Your Codex or Claude Code does the agent work; you use the same directory and application records.

## What you need

- A computer with [Node.js 24 or newer](https://nodejs.org/en/download).
- One installed, signed-in **Codex or Claude Code**.
- Your real CV information. You can create or connect your Tabbio CV during setup.

The app is free. Your assistant account and connected services may have their own plans or usage costs.

## Start with one request

1. Open [Get started](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/FIRST-RUN.md) and download the app’s source ZIP, then extract it. A country directory ZIP is an add-on, not the app.
2. Open the extracted Tawdheef folder in your chosen assistant.
3. Paste the [setup request](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/FIRST-RUN.md#give-your-agent-one-setup-request). Your agent handles the local commands and guides you through the sign-ins that need you.

[setup-prompt]: #

When the local app opens, complete your sign-ins in **Connections**. Choose your assistant there and use **Check connections**. First launch needs internet to install the project dependencies. [Get started](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef/blob/main/docs/FIRST-RUN.md) also has the manual commands.

## Expected result

The tracker opens on your computer. Your agent reports **Ready** or gives you one concrete next step, such as signing in or connecting your CV. Setup does not start applications.
