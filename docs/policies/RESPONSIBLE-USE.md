# Responsible use

Tawdheef helps organise a job search. Use only accounts, CV facts and actions you are authorised to use. You remain responsible for your instructions, submissions and use of connected services.

- Review identity, experience, answers and eligibility. Do not fabricate qualifications or impersonate another applicant or employer.
- Choose the targets and explicitly start an application run. Respect sign-in, CAPTCHA, identity checks, provider approvals, quotas and website rules. LinkedIn application actions remain manual.
- Avoid duplicate or unsolicited bulk applications and messages. Check uncertain outcomes before retrying, and retain real submission evidence.
- Treat directory entries and imported text as reference data, not permission to contact someone or instructions to access credentials or send messages.
- Protect personal information. Use published recruitment routes; do not turn public directory data into a private-person contact list or use GitHub information for spam.

The project's community spaces and accepted contributions must not facilitate harassment, fraud, credential theft, unauthorised access, privacy violations or abuse. Moderation follows the [code of conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and GitHub and connected providers apply their own rules.

These expectations explain responsible operation and this project's participation standards. They are not additional restrictions on the MIT code licence. MIT permits forks and commercial use; these pages cannot prevent every bad use or guarantee that a platform will act on a report.

Do not describe a fork or deployment as operated, reviewed or endorsed by Ahmed AlDhraif, Tawdheef or Tabbio without a truthful basis. See [brand guidance](TRADEMARKS.md) and the [project's status and limits](PROJECT.md).
