# Apply and build career profiles

You choose companies and actions. Your selected client performs external work; Tawdheef records scope and evidence.

## Before you start

Connect the actual client and CV through [Connections](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef-auto-apply-jobs/blob/main/docs/guides/use-the-skill.md#agent). Ask it to search careers pages with your real CV and preferences. Review the Jobs list. A blocked page is not proof of no vacancies.

## Choose companies and actions

Filter or select companies, then open **Run** and review its targets:

- Matching applications: only roles supported by your experience and eligibility.
- Career profiles: reuse or complete accounts, profiles, uploads and talent pools where supported, independently of applications.
- CV-only email: a separate optional choice, off by default, for verified published careers routes.

## Start the run

Run saves the scope and copies a request. Paste it into your selected client. Tawdheef waits for that client to claim work; it does not launch a new model or CLI process.

The client uses its own browser, mail and password-manager access. You handle unlock, sign-in, CAPTCHA and provider approvals. LinkedIn actions remain manual.

## Track real outcomes

An account, profile or CV upload is not an application. Record each separately. Applications and CV emails need real receipts. Inspect uncertain outcomes before retrying to avoid duplicates.

## Stop or retry

Stop revokes local authority. Stop remaining work in your client too. New actions need a fresh explicit Run; reconcile uncertain receipts first.

## Monitor new jobs {#monitor-new-jobs}

Use [read-only monitoring](https://github.com/ahmedaldhraif/tawdheef-auto-apply-jobs/blob/main/docs/guides/replies-and-reports.md#monitor-new-jobs). Monitoring never authorizes applications or profile work.
