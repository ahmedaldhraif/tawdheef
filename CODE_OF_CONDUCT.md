# Community conduct

People from any country and background are welcome to contribute. Be respectful, specific and useful. Discuss the work; do not attack the person.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed community spaces, including interactions when representing the project. It does not add restrictions to the MIT software licence or control independent forks.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate, spam, share private information without permission, or submit material you do not have the right to share. Keep reports and examples relevant, factual and free of credentials, CVs and private applicant data. Respect a maintainer's request to move or end a discussion.

The personal maintainer, Ahmed AlDhraif, may clarify expectations, edit or remove project-space content, close submissions, or limit participation where appropriate. Decisions concern community participation, not cancellation of rights under the code licence. The maintainer cannot promise continuous moderation, a fixed response time or control of other people's copies.

For an ordinary conduct concern, point to the public interaction with minimal detail. If a report involves private information, ask for a private reporting route without publishing it. Report platform abuse through GitHub's reporting tools. Security or sensitive data exposures should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

See [project status and policy limits](docs/policies/PROJECT.md). No confidential contact inbox or reporting service is implied by this file.
